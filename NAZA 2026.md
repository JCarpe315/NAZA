**World premiere:** September 10, 2026

**Plot:** Filmed at night on rooftops in Tel Aviv, the documentary records testimony from Israeli military and intelligence personnel about the targeting of Gaza. Their identities are concealed as they describe surveillance, the selection of buildings for airstrikes, and the calculation of anticipated civilian deaths. The title refers to the military shorthand used for those projected casualties. Interviewees explain how computer systems and bureaucratic procedures turn individual lives into numbers, while the filmmakers question the assumptions behind the process. The accounts extend investigative reporting by the Guardian, +972 Magazine, and Local Call, placing the speakers' recollections alongside the consequences of military decisions. Rather than following one protagonist, the film assembles an examination of institutional responsibility through conversations about orders, knowledge, and participation.
