NAZA (2026) — Complete Transcript
The complete, verified transcript of NAZA (2026), the award-winning documentary by Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor (No Other Land). Premiered at the Venice Film Festival, September 10, 2026.
The title is Israeli military slang: in the IDF, "NAZA" is the number of civilians expected to die in an airstrike. The film interviews 24 anonymous Israeli soldiers and intelligence officers — on Tel Aviv rooftops, at night — about how that number is calculated, and about the systems behind it: the Lavender AI target-marking program, "Where's Daddy" family-phone alerts, "night location" home-mapping, mass home demolition, sniper fire on food distribution lines, and "Bibi's department," the political intelligence unit that spied on war-crimes petitioners to keep cases from reaching The Hague.
Why this exists
